Sales Development Representative (SDR) / Business Development Associate (BDA)

Bengaluru (Indiranagar)
Full-Time
1–2 years experience

Role Overview

As a Sales Development Representative / Business Development Associate at Lumos, you will be the driving force behind our pipeline growth. You will identify high-potential B2B, D2C accounts, engage key decision-makers across media, brand, and corporate communications sectors, and qualify sales leads. Working directly with the account execution and sales leadership teams, you will turn cold outreach into valuable enterprise conversations.

Core Responsibilities & Sales Execution

Lead Generation & Prospecting: Identify, target, and map prospective enterprise clients using LinkedIn, other databases, and market research.

Market Intelligence: Track industry trends, competitor activities, and news events to craft timely, hyper-relevant outreach messaging.

Outbound Outreach: Build and execute multi-channel cold outreach campaigns (email, phone, direct selling) to decision-makers (CMOs, PR/Communications Directors, Brand Managers).

Inbound Lead Qualification: Promptly qualify inbound inquiries to assess budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT framework) before transferring leads to Account Executives.

Scheduling Meetings: Secure qualified product discovery meetings and live demos for the sales leadership team.

Data Management: Maintain accurate data, log outreach activities, track prospect status, and update deal pipelines daily.

Qualifications & Experience

Work Experience: 1–2 years of experience in B2B sales, SaaS sales, or technology solution selling (SDR, BDA, or Inside Sales roles).

Track Record: Proven ability to meet or exceed monthly/quarterly activity metrics and qualified meeting targets.

Domain Knowledge: Familiarity with social intelligence, PR tech, media monitoring, or SaaS platforms is a strong plus.

Skills & Competencies

Communication: Exceptional written, verbal, and interpersonal communication skills; active listener.

Sales Aptitude: Strong objection-handling ability, persuasion skills, and persistent follow-up discipline.

Tech-Savvy: Proficiency with modern sales tools (CRM like HubSpot/Salesforce, outreach tools like Lemlist/Apollo, and LinkedIn Sales Navigator).

Education

Bachelor’s degree or Master's Degree in Marketing, Sales, Business development, MBA, Marketing Management, Degree in International Business development.

What We Offer

Competitive Compensation

Career Growth: Clear progression paths toward various senior roles. An opportunity to shape the future of a pioneering, high-growth organization.

Continuous Learning: Hands-on training on enterprise B2B SaaS strategies, media intelligence tools, and modern sales tech.

Culture & Perks: A collaborative, forward-thinking work environment with significant autonomy with sales incentives.
